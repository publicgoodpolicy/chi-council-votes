## Conventions (ruled by Ishan 2026-09-01)

- A ratified candidate consumes the next free number **at transcription**, not at ratification; the number is allocated here and nowhere else.
- A **dismissed** or **declined** candidate consumes nothing; it is recorded without a number in the dispositions section so the finding is not lost.
- **Executor-class** instances stay off this ledger; the executor owns them in its own reports and the handover's lane record.
- Entry form: a `### N` heading; the entry text; a bracketed trailer stating the source identity and span (when transcribed) or the drafting artifact (when authored fresh), the ratification date, and the allocation date. Text copied from a source is copied byte for byte and never edited inside; anything an entry must say beyond its source goes in the trailer.
- The planner drafts entries and never ratifies its own; every number below was ratified by Ishan.
